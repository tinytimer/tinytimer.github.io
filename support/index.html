Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

How do I start a timer?+

Choose Start a new timer, select a duration, promise, reveal picture, and reveal style, then tap Start timer. You can pause, resume, restart, or stop at any time.

Where are my routines and photos stored?+

Routines, phrases, preferences, and imported photos stay on the device where you created them. They are isolated by your parent account and are not uploaded to Tiny Timer's servers.

How do I restore Premium?+

Sign in to the same Tiny Timer account, open Account Settings, and tap Restore purchases. You must also be signed in to the Apple or Google store account that originally purchased the subscription.

How do I cancel or manage a subscription?+

Open Account Settings and tap Manage subscription. Billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are controlled by Apple or Google. Deleting your Tiny Timer account does not cancel a store subscription.

How do I delete my account and data?+

Open Account Settings, choose Delete account, and follow the confirmation steps. This deletes your remote parent account and Tiny Timer data stored for that account on the device. Cancel Premium separately through your store account if you do not want it to renew.

The timer notification did not appear. What should I check?+

Confirm notifications are allowed for Tiny Timer in your device settings. If Focus or Do Not Disturb is active, your device may silence the completion alert even though the timer continues in the app.

Your privacy choices

You can change marketing email preferences and delete your account from Account Settings in the app. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
